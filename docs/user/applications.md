# Applications and approval

## For members

1. Sign in and open **Community → Membership** (or **Apply for membership** on your dashboard).
2. Fill in your details as the school would have them (name, batch, section…). Add proof if you have it: a registration number, or a photo or PDF of a certificate or old ID card. Only administrators see it.
3. Press **Apply**. The page then shows where your application is:
   - **Waiting for a decision**;
   - **On hold**: the administrator needs more. Their note says what; update the form and press **Send again**;
   - **Approved**: you're a member;
   - **Not accepted**: the note says why. You can apply again with more details.

## For administrators

Open **Community → Applications**. The **Waiting** tab lists applications that need a decision.

Open one to see what the person sent, their proof, and **matching records**: people in your data with the same email, phone, or batch and name.

- **Approve:**
  - pick the matching record to link their account to it (your data isn't changed), or
  - choose "None of these" (or there's no match) to add them as a new record.
- **Hold:** ask for more. Write what you need in the note; they can update and send it again.
- **Reject:** write the reason in the note; they see it.

The history at the bottom shows every change, who made it, and the note.
